# Code of conduct

Diffusers Engine Q21 contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming,
professional, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and security disclosure boundaries.
- Do not post private images, credentials, personal information, or harmful
  content in issues, pull requests, benchmark reports, or discussions.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disclosure
of private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly submitting material
without redistribution rights are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces managed by the project.
